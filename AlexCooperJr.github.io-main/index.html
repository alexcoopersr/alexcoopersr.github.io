About

More than inventory.

My background spans inventory control, supply chain operations, relationship building, procurement, hands-on work, and problem-solving.

My work focuses on the relationship between inventory, operations, and the people who keep a supply chain moving. I enjoy understanding how materials, information, and resources flow through an organization and identifying ways to make those processes more efficient.

My experience spans inventory management, procurement, customer service, and hands-on operational work. These experiences have taught me the importance of organization, attention to detail, accurate information, and clear communication when managing inventory and supporting day-to-day operations.

I bring a practical approach to problem-solving, with an emphasis on understanding the issue, identifying its cause, and working toward a reliable solution. Whether coordinating with others, managing inventory, or addressing an operational challenge, I value accuracy, accountability, and getting the job done right.

Beyond inventory control, I am interested in the systems and processes that support supply chain operations. I enjoy learning how different parts of an organization work together and finding opportunities to improve efficiency, communication, and the way work gets done.
